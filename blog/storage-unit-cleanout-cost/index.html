Clearing Out a Storage Unit: What It Costs and How to Do It Fast

Published August 20, 2026 · DJR Junk Removal · Meridian, Boise & the Treasure Valley

A storage unit is the only bill that grows quieter the longer you ignore it. Most people who call us about one have been paying on it for years and have not opened it in months.

Do the math first — it usually decides it for you

Take your monthly rate and multiply by twelve. Then ask honestly what you would pay, today, to buy everything in that unit brand new. For a lot of units the annual rent has quietly passed the replacement cost of the contents, sometimes several times over.

That is not an argument for throwing everything away. It is an argument for opening the door and finding out, because the meter is running either way.

What a cleanout costs

Priced by volume, same as any job. A 5x10 unit packed floor to ceiling is usually a half to three-quarter load ($275–$389). A 10x10 is commonly a full load ($495) or slightly over. A unit that is half-empty or holds a few large items often lands at the minimum or a quarter ($80–$145).

If you can send a photo from the doorway with the roll-up open, that answers the question faster than any description.

How to work through one quickly

  1. Book the pickup before you start sorting. Same principle as a garage. A deadline is what turns this from a project into an afternoon.
  2. Work from the door inward, in a single pass. Do not excavate to the back first and then start over.
  3. Three piles only: keep, worth selling, gone. A fourth pile is how people end up renting a second unit.
  4. Open every box. The whole reason the unit exists is that somebody once decided not to. It takes two minutes a box and it is where the actual decisions are.

The paperwork trap. Old tax records, bank statements and medical paperwork are common in storage units and should not just go in a truck. Set those aside for shredding. Anything with an account number on it deserves two extra minutes.

Close the unit properly

Sweep it out, take photos of the empty unit, and get written confirmation that your contract is terminated. Facilities generally require the unit broom-clean, and a verbal "you are all set" is not the same as being off the billing. This is the step people skip and then find another month on the card.
