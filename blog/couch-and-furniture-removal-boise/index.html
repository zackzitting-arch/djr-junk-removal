Getting Rid of a Couch, Sectional or Bedroom Set in Boise

Published November 3, 2026 · DJR Junk Removal · Meridian, Boise & the Treasure Valley

Furniture is awkward for a specific reason: it is too big for the trash, too heavy for one person, and usually too worn to sell. So it sits in the garage for a year. Here is the short version of what to do with it.

First, be honest about the condition

A clean, solid piece with no damage has a second life, and it is worth ten minutes trying to find it. A piece that is stained, broken, sagging or has lived with a pet does not. Donation centers are polite about it, but a rejected drop-off means you loaded a couch into a truck for nothing.

If it is genuinely good

A free listing with "must pick up, first come" clears in a day or two in Boise and Meridian, and the buyer supplies the labor. Some donation centers in the valley will collect larger pieces — call first and describe it accurately.

If it is not

That is what we do. It goes on the truck with whatever else you have, we sort out anything usable afterward, and the rest goes where it should.

What it costs

Furniture is priced by the space it takes up in the truck, like everything else. One or two pieces usually land at or near the $125 minimum. A bedroom set — bed frame, mattress and box spring, dresser, nightstands — is typically a quarter load ($290). A living room's worth, or a whole apartment's furniture, climbs into the half and three-quarter tiers ($500–$630).

Tell us what the pieces are and where they are sitting, and you get a range before we come out. The crew confirms the price on site before loading.

Sleeper sofas and sectionals are the ones to warn us about. A sleeper has a steel bed mechanism inside and weighs roughly twice what it looks like. A sectional is several pieces that may not have fit through the door assembled, and may not fit out that way either. Neither is a problem, both are easier with a heads-up.

Do not do this

Do not put a couch at the curb hoping the city will take it or someone will grab it. Most curbside services in Ada County will not collect bulky furniture on a regular pickup, and a couch on the parkway for a week is the kind of thing neighbors report. Do not try to carry a dresser down the stairs alone, either — it is the most common way people get hurt doing their own cleanout.

Mattresses have their own quirks; see mattress disposal in Boise.
