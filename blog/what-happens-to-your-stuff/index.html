What Actually Happens to Your Stuff After We Haul It

Published August 31, 2026 · DJR Junk Removal · Meridian, Boise & the Treasure Valley

It is the question we get asked most often once the truck is loaded, and it is a fair one. The honest answer is that a load gets sorted, not dumped.

A load is not one thing

A garage cleanout is furniture, metal, electronics, cardboard, yard waste and genuine trash all mixed together. Those have completely different destinations, which is why sorting happens after pickup rather than at your curb — it is faster for you and it is the only practical way to do it.

The four paths

Donation

Usable furniture, working appliances, tools, household goods. The bar is whether someone would actually want it — a couch with a stain and a broken frame does not help a charity, it costs them money to dispose of. We would rather be honest about that than pretend.

Recycling

Metal is the big one and it is genuinely worth recycling. Appliances, bed frames, filing cabinets, fencing, tools. Cardboard and clean wood have paths too, and electronics go through their own stream.

Specialty handling

Anything with refrigerant, mattresses, tires and electronics all have their own requirements — that is the category with rules attached, and it is why we ask what is in the load before we arrive.

The landfill

What is left. Broken particleboard furniture, contaminated material, general household waste. This is a real share of most loads and anyone claiming otherwise is selling something.

You can make the sorting work better. If you have genuinely good items you want to see donated, set them aside and tell us. It takes ten seconds and it means the good stuff does not end up buried under the demolition debris in the truck.

What we cannot take, and why

Paint, chemicals, fuel, asbestos and medical waste are excluded by regulation, not by preference. Those go through household hazardous waste channels. See what junk removal can and cannot take for the full list, and check Ada County for current drop-off options.

Why it matters to you

Beyond the obvious: sorting is also why volume pricing works. A hauler who dumps everything pays landfill rates on the whole load. Sorting keeps the cost structure sane, which is what lets a half load stay at $275 instead of drifting upward.
