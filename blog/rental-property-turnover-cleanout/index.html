Clearing a Rental Between Tenants, Fast

Published August 27, 2026 · DJR Junk Removal · Meridian, Boise & the Treasure Valley

For a landlord the math on this is simple and unforgiving: a unit full of the last tenant's belongings is a unit earning nothing.

Know the rules before you touch anything

Abandoned property is a legal question, not a hauling question. Idaho has specific requirements around notice and handling when a tenant leaves belongings behind, and they depend on how the tenancy ended.

Get that settled before anything goes in a truck. If there is any doubt about whether the tenancy is properly terminated or whether the property was abandoned, talk to your attorney or property manager first. We are not a substitute for that step, and no reputable hauler will tell you otherwise.

Once you are clear to proceed

  1. Photograph everything before it moves. Wide shots of every room, plus close-ups of any damage. This is your record for the deposit and for anything that comes later.
  2. Separate obvious personal documents. IDs, paperwork, photographs. Even in a clean-cut abandonment, that material deserves different handling.
  3. Then clear it in one pass. Furniture, appliances, trash, the garage, the patio.

What it costs and how long it takes

A studio or one-bedroom left partially furnished is usually a half load ($275). A fully furnished two or three bedroom is commonly a full load ($495) or more. Most turnovers are a single visit.

The bottleneck is almost never the hauling — it is the days that pass before someone schedules it.

Book it for the day the notice period ends. Not the week after. The single biggest cost in a turnover is vacancy, and every part of the process after the cleanout — paint, carpet, cleaning, listing — is waiting on the unit being empty.

Working with property managers

We can meet a manager or a maintenance tech on site, work from a lockbox with authorization, and turn a unit around without the owner needing to be there. If you manage several properties in Meridian, Boise or the surrounding area, tell us — it is easier for everyone if we know your units.
