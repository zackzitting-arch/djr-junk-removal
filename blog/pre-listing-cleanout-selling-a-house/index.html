Selling a House? Get It Empty Before the Photos

Published November 16, 2026 · DJR Junk Removal · Meridian, Boise & the Treasure Valley

Buyers see your house twice before they ever walk in: once in the listing photos, once on a drive-by. Both are better when the garage closes, the side yard is clear and the spare room looks like a room.

Three cleanouts, not one

Before the photos

This is the big one. Clear the garage so it reads as a garage. Empty the closets to half full — buyers open them. Get the exercise equipment out of the bedroom and the collection of planters off the patio. Anything that will not be in the house when you move does not need to be in the photos.

During showings

Keep a box in the car for the last-minute stuff. This is about tidiness, not hauling.

Before the walk-through

The contract almost always requires the house empty and broom clean at closing. Leftover paint cans in the garage, a broken swing set, the shelves you decided not to take — anything still there on walk-through day becomes a negotiation, and the buyer's number for removing it is always higher than ours.

Agents: this is the call to make at the listing appointment. A cleanout booked the week before photos is routine. A cleanout booked the morning of the walk-through is a scramble, and the seller's leftover pile has sunk more than one closing timeline. We can meet a seller, a stager or you with a lockbox code — whatever gets it done.

What usually goes

Garage contents, old furniture, the shed, the second fridge, the hot tub that no longer runs, yard debris and the pile behind the fence. Working appliances and clean furniture are set aside for donation in the sort; the rest is handled properly. See what actually happens to your stuff.

What it costs

By volume, like every other job, $125 minimum up to $695 for a full truck. A pre-photo tidy — the garage and a few rooms' worth — is commonly a half load ($500). A house that has been lived in hard for twenty years is often a full load or more than one trip. Send photos of each area and you get a range before we come out; the price is confirmed on site before anything is loaded.

Timing

Book the main cleanout for the week before the photographer, not the day before — it leaves room to clean and touch up once the stuff is out. Book the final clear for the day after the movers, before the walk-through. Moving and cleanout on the same day is possible but it is a long day; see what to do with everything you are not taking.

Listing a house soon?

Send photos of the garage and any rooms that need clearing. We will get you a range, usually within the hour.
