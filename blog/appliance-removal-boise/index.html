Appliance Removal: Fridges, Washers and What Happens Next

Published August 25, 2026 · DJR Junk Removal · Meridian, Boise & the Treasure Valley

Appliances are heavy, awkward, and — in the case of anything that cools — legally different from the rest of your junk.

Anything with refrigerant is a special case

Refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners, dehumidifiers and wine coolers all contain refrigerant, and it cannot simply go to a landfill. It has to be recovered by someone certified to do it before the unit is scrapped.

That is not a reason to worry about it — it is routine, and it is handled. It is just a reason to tell us what you have when you book, because it changes where the unit goes after it leaves your house.

Before we arrive

  • Empty it. Especially freezers. A chest freezer that has been off for a week is a genuinely unpleasant surprise for everyone.
  • Disconnect it. Unplug it, shut off the water line to a fridge or washer, and shut off the gas to a dryer or range. If you are not confident with the gas connection, have a plumber or the utility handle it — that is not a DIY line.
  • Drain it. Washers and water heaters hold more water than people expect, and it ends up on your floor otherwise.
  • Clear the route. Doors, tight hallways, stairs. Tell us in advance if it is coming out of a basement.

Take the doors off, or do not leave it accessible. An old fridge or freezer sitting outside with a working door latch is a genuine child-safety hazard. If it is going to sit in the driveway overnight before pickup, remove the door.

What it costs

A single appliance is usually a minimum load ($80). A kitchen's worth during a remodel — fridge, range, dishwasher, microwave — is typically a quarter to half load ($145–$275), and the old cabinets and countertop usually come with it.

If it still works

Try to move it before you scrap it. A working appliance has real value to someone, and a free listing with "must pick up" often clears in a day. Utilities sometimes run recycling or rebate programs for old, inefficient units too — worth checking before you decide.

If it does not work, or nobody takes it, that is what we are for.
