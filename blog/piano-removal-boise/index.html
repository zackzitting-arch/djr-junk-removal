Piano Removal: Why It Is Its Own Job

Published November 10, 2026 · DJR Junk Removal · Meridian, Boise & the Treasure Valley

The old upright has been in the family for forty years, nobody has played it in twenty, and it turns out the thing that made it valuable to your grandmother makes it nearly impossible to give away today.

The uncomfortable truth about old pianos

There are far more used pianos than people who want them. Unless the instrument is a recognized brand in good playing condition, the resale value is close to zero, and even a free listing often goes unanswered because the buyer has to move it. Piano stores, schools and churches get offered them constantly. Try for a week if it matters to you — then make the decision.

Why it cannot be moved like a couch

An upright weighs several hundred pounds, a grand considerably more, and almost all of that weight is in a cast-iron plate near the top of the case. That makes it top-heavy and prone to tipping, and the legs and casters on an old piano are the weakest part of it. Dragging one across a hardwood floor, or trying to walk it down two steps with three friends, is how floors get gouged and backs get hurt.

Moving it properly takes a piano board or heavy dolly, straps, and people who have done it. That is what we show up with.

Tell us which kind it is and where it sits. Upright, console, spinet or grand; ground floor or up a flight; through a standard door or a tight hallway. A grand usually has its legs and lyre removed before it moves, which is part of the job. All of this changes how we plan the visit, so it is the first thing we will ask.

What happens to it

An instrument that genuinely works has a chance at a second home, and if you tell us it still plays we will say so when the load is sorted. Most old uprights are past that point. The cast-iron plate and the steel strings are recyclable; the wood case and felts are not, and that part is disposed of properly.

What it costs

A piano is one of the few items we do not price purely by truck space — the weight and the handling are the job, not the volume. On its own it is typically priced above the $125 minimum, and the range depends mostly on the type and the access. As part of a larger cleanout it is added to the load. Describe it, send a photo of the piano and the route out, and you get a range before we come; the price is confirmed on site before anything moves.

Got a piano that needs to go?

Send a photo of the piano and the doorway it has to leave through. We will get you a range, usually within the hour.
