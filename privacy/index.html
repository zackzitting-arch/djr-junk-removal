By using our website or services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1.Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following information:

Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service address or general location
  • Billing or payment information
  • Information about the junk removal services you are requesting
  • Photos you submit for estimates or quotes
  • Information provided through contact forms, quote forms, or scheduling requests
  • Communications you have with DJR Junk Removal

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and device information
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of your visit
  • Referring website
  • General website usage information

This information may be collected through cookies, analytics tools, or similar technologies.

2.How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, schedule, and manage junk removal services
  • Provide estimates and quotes
  • Communicate with you about your request or appointment
  • Process payments
  • Send appointment confirmations, reminders, and service-related messages
  • Respond to questions and customer service requests
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Analyze website traffic and usage
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Maintain business and accounting records
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations

We may also use your contact information to send promotional or marketing communications where permitted by law. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions provided in the communication or by contacting us.

3.Photos and Quote Information

If you submit photographs of junk, furniture, property, or other items to DJR Junk Removal for the purpose of obtaining an estimate or quote, we may use those photographs to evaluate the requested service and provide pricing.

We may retain submitted photographs and related quote information for legitimate business purposes, including maintaining customer records, resolving disputes, improving estimating practices, and documenting services.

We will not publicly post photographs containing identifiable personal information without appropriate permission.

4.Social Media and Marketing Photography

DJR Junk Removal may use photographs and other visual content for marketing, advertising, educational, and promotional purposes, including on our website, Instagram, Facebook, Google Business Profile, and other social media or marketing platforms.

Photographs may include:

  • Photos submitted by customers for estimates or quotes
  • Before-and-after photos taken by DJR Junk Removal during or after a service
  • Photos of items, loads, vehicles, or completed work
  • Other photographs taken in connection with providing our services

Customer Privacy

DJR Junk Removal is committed to protecting the privacy of our customers. We will make reasonable efforts to ensure that photographs used for public marketing do not unnecessarily reveal private or identifying information.

We will not intentionally publish photographs that reveal sensitive or private customer information, including:

  • Customer names or contact information
  • Addresses or identifiable house numbers
  • Documents containing personal information
  • Personal financial or payment information
  • Other information that could reasonably compromise a customer’s privacy or security

Before-and-after photographs may be taken at a customer’s property for documentation and service-related purposes. However, DJR Junk Removal will not use such photographs publicly in a manner that could reasonably identify or compromise the privacy of the customer without the customer’s approval.

Customer Approval

When a photograph could reasonably identify a customer, their property, or other private information, DJR Junk Removal will seek the customer’s approval before using the photograph for public marketing or social media purposes.

Customer approval may be provided electronically, in writing, by text message, email, or through another reasonable method that allows DJR Junk Removal to document the customer’s permission.

A customer may decline permission to have photographs used for public marketing. Declining permission will not affect the customer’s ability to receive services from DJR Junk Removal.

If a customer has previously approved the use of a photograph and later requests that DJR Junk Removal discontinue its use, we will make reasonable efforts to honor the request for future use. Because content may be shared, reposted, or cached by third parties, we cannot guarantee that we can remove every copy that may exist outside of accounts or platforms controlled by DJR Junk Removal.

Customer-Submitted Photos

Customers may voluntarily provide photographs to DJR Junk Removal for the purpose of obtaining estimates, quotes, or arranging services. Those photographs may be used internally to evaluate the requested service.

If DJR Junk Removal wishes to use a customer-submitted photograph for public marketing or social media purposes, we will seek appropriate customer approval when the photograph could reasonably identify the customer, their property, or private information.

No Compromise of Customer Privacy

Our goal is to showcase the work we perform while respecting the privacy and security of the people we serve. DJR Junk Removal will use reasonable judgment when selecting photographs for public use and will avoid publishing content that unnecessarily exposes a customer’s private information.

Nothing in this section prevents DJR Junk Removal from using photographs for legitimate internal business purposes, such as estimating, documenting completed work, training, quality control, customer service, or resolving disputes.

5.Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment processors. DJR Junk Removal generally does not directly store complete credit card or debit card numbers on its own systems.

Payment information may be collected and processed by the applicable payment provider in accordance with that provider’s privacy policy and security practices.

6.Service Providers and Third Parties

We may use third-party companies and service providers to help operate our business, including providers for:

  • Website hosting
  • Customer relationship management
  • Scheduling
  • Estimates and invoicing
  • Payment processing
  • Accounting
  • Website analytics
  • Communications and text messaging
  • Advertising and marketing

These providers may have access to personal information when reasonably necessary to perform services on our behalf. We expect our service providers to handle information appropriately and in accordance with applicable laws and their contractual obligations.

We do not sell your personal information as a business practice.

7.Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand how visitors use our website, remember preferences, and measure advertising or website performance.

Third-party analytics or advertising providers may also use cookies or similar technologies.

You may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of portions of our website.

8.Text Messages and Phone Communications

If you provide your phone number and request services or communication from DJR Junk Removal, we may contact you by phone call or text message regarding your request, appointment, estimate, payment, or other service-related matters.

Where required by law, we will obtain appropriate consent before sending marketing text messages.

You may opt out of marketing text messages by following the instructions provided in the message or contacting DJR Junk Removal directly. Opting out of marketing messages does not necessarily prevent us from sending service-related communications that are necessary to fulfill a transaction or appointment.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

9.How We Share Information

We may disclose personal information in the following circumstances:

  • To employees and contractors who need the information to provide our services
  • To service providers who assist with our business operations
  • To payment processors and financial service providers
  • To accounting, legal, or professional advisors
  • When necessary to comply with a legal obligation, court order, subpoena, or governmental request
  • When necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of DJR Junk Removal, our customers, or others
  • In connection with a business transfer, merger, sale, acquisition, or similar transaction

We do not sell or rent customer personal information to third parties.

10.Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no website, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted to or stored by us.

11.Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and financial records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, comply with legal obligations, and fulfill other legitimate business purposes.

The length of time information is retained may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

12.Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not directed toward children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 18 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

13.Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties. DJR Junk Removal is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

14.Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, which may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we maintain about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain personal information
  • Opt out of certain marketing communications
  • Ask questions about how your information is collected or used

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the information provided below.

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.
